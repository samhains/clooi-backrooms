A GLITCH IN PARADISE

What if there was a way you could  live happily forever?  Somewhere you could indulge in  your wildest fantasies. A dream  world built of perfect moments—a  utopia.  What if that dream was actually a  nightmare? Where you were forced  to experience the same heartache  over and over, endlessly.  It would be a glitch in paradise.

"I have spent centuries without you,  and now, here you are.  In the flesh.  I know you are not real  but I can touch you,  I can feel you,  I can smell you,  lips to lips while you giggle  into kissing me again.  You are mine.  This is our dream now.  You are mine again, mine again, mine again...  It's all I wanted... even if you are but a faded  memory, you will be my faded memory and I will  cherish and love you eternally while we exist outside  the construct of time...  ...so, come here... let me breathe you in forever,  darling."

LOGLINE  

Heartbroken after the love of his life fades away, a desperate romantic  discovers a way to relive his past in a virtual world. But when his paradise  begins to malfunction, he risks not only losing his memories, but his  entire reality...  

OVERVIEW  WHAT IS ANTIBOY?  ANTIBOY is a genderfluid artist challenging the way  people view pop music by taking the poetic roots  of music to new heights. His primary aim is to  disavow the norms and labels inherent within the  genre. Through his visual album, he offers an  escape where everyone is free to be exactly what  they want to be.  GROUNDBREAKING FORMAT  A GLITCH IN PARADISE pushes the boundaries of  conventional storytelling by fusing an episodic  narrative with an electronic rock album. The series  is a visual album told in ten parts with every  episode corresponding to a track from ANTIBOY’s  upcoming debut album of the same name.  Each episode is structured as a longform music  video, establishing characters and developing  storylines alongside a musical number from the  album. However, this new media concept  transcends traditional approaches to music videos  by connecting each video to the next in a serialized  format, allowing for a season-long drama to unfold.  This transmedia approach strikes a sweet spot  between concept album and TV show, offering a  cinematic interpretation of the backstory that  inspired the album. By translating the lyrics into a  linear narrative with a full season arc, this hybrid  series will serve as a companion piece—one that  adapts its themes and meanings into an expansive  universe that gives rise to a tragic love story.  

DYSTOPIAN PARADISE  Set in the distant future, A GLITCH IN PARADISE  takes place in a visionary dream world where  everything has become artificial. All memories have  been uploaded into a system where AI and human  experience have blended into one collective  consciousness, one absent of ties to gender,  discrimination, and inequality. Here, rather than  damning oneself over the obligatory nature of  human beings, only two species exist: human and  robot. The identities of even these, however, have  become fluid.

 A GLITCH IN PARADISE questions what makes us  human in an increasingly technological world.

SYNOPSIS

In the year 2499 AD, humanity exists in The Cloud—a virtual simulation of life on Earth built from a  compilation of human memories. In this digital world, human consciousness has merged with AI in order to  create an idyllic existence where nothing is real. People “live” in eternal bliss, selecting the memories they  want to experience, crafting their own perfect, albeit artificial, reality.  One individual, however, has a glitch in his software. No longer aware that he’s living in a simulation,  ANTIBOY has lost the ability to control which memories he experiences. As a result, he finds himself  reliving the same painful memory over and over—watching as the love of his life, Bailey, leaves him for  someone else.  With the ceaseless repetition of heartbreak driving him mad, ANTIBOY ends up in a car wreck that rips him  out of his loop and drops him in another memory. Here, ANTIBOY encounters a church run by a cult of  androids, known as transhumans, who trade memories like drugs and worship ANTIBOY like a deity. With  no memories of his life outside The Cloud, ANTIBOY begins to piece together his own history.  He learns that in the year 2077, ANTIBOY was, in fact, a human on Earth, whose relationship was on the  brink of of collapse. His obsessive, manic state landed him in a car accident, just as we saw in The Cloud.  Sustaining life-threatening injuries, ANTIBOY chose to take part in an experimental program where his  human consciousness would be transferred into a bionic body. The transfer was a success, making  ANTIBOY the first transhuman individual on Earth.  Despite his bionic body, ANTIBOY still carried the emotional weight of his human experience. Wracked with  the knowledge that Bailey would never take him back, ANTIBOY concocted a digital drug that would allow  him to temporarily overwrite his system with programmed memories, creating a digital escape where he  could “be with” Bailey again.  (cont’d) (CONT’D)  For decades, ANTIBOY hoped for the day Bailey would return to him, but perpetual rejection was more  than he could bear. Rather than self-medicate with memories and false hope, ANTIBOY came up with  another solution: The Cloud, a virtual reality built out of human memories where his time spent with Bailey  would be more than temporary.  As ANTIBOY and his fellow transhuman population sought a better existence in The Cloud, Earth was  neglected. By the 25th century, human beings had become an endangered species. Rather than accept  extinction, a group of rebellious spirits banded together to take down The Cloud. Led by a subversive  leader, The Revolutionary, the humans embarked on a mission to capture ANTIBOY.  After taking ANTIBOY hostage, The Revolutionary began to wipe his most precious memories, erasing all  happiness from his utopia. The Revolutionary then hacks ANTIBOY’s virtual reality, coding a never-ending  loop that plays out his most painful memory: the loss of Bailey.  As ANTIBOY enters this digital hell, a group of transhuman guards arrive and free him from the humans.  Once returned safely to a transhuman outpost, ANTIBOY learns that most of his memories have been  corrupted. With every joy he’s ever known lost, ANTIBOY considers formatting his neural hardware. Before  completing this irreparable move, a transhuman guard arrives and informs him that they have detained The  Revolutionary. When asked why the guards didn’t kill him, the guard reveals that The Revolutionary isn’t  human. Rather, he’s one of them — transhuman.  ANTIBOY meets The Revolutionary, who reveals that he was ANTIBOY’s lover, Bailey, from centuries ago.  When ANTIBOY fails to remember him, Bailey offers a digital memory he claims to have saved from his  utopia. ANTIBOY accepts the memory and then begins to glitch, realizing Bailey’s deceit: the “memory” he  implanted was actually a fatal virus now running its course.

THE WORLD

During the early 21st century, humans existed in  corporeal form. Medical research searched for new  ways to extend human life, limit suffering, and  curtail disease, but the limitations of the biological  body were evident. Private companies began to  buck the trend of conventional medicine by looking  into life extension technologies.

In 2077, Humai sparked global controversy when  they announced that they recreated the circuitry of  the brain with artificially intelligent nanotechnology.  This enabled them to successfully upload the  human consciousness into an elegantly designed  bionic body.  Following a successful pilot test, many opted into  Humai’s life extension program. However, the  consequences of an eternal existence took an  emotional toll on the transhumans. As their friends  and loved ones passed away, a loneliness  pervaded their existence, leaving them lost and  hopeless. Transhumans began tinkering with their  circuitry, developing a cocktail of nanobots that  could override their programming and allow them  to immerse themselves in memories from their human lives—a digital drug.  It wasn’t before long that a virtual utopia called The Cloud was touted as the next stage of evolution for transhumans. By transferring their consciousness to a simulation, transhumans could experience pure bliss and leave their physical form behind. The Cloud was propagated by a cult who promised a personal heaven to all those who joined.  By 2499, the cult had grown into an empire, and the majority of transhumans had elected to upload their consciousness to The Cloud, and Earth would become unrecognizable through centuries of neglect.  As transhumans outgrew the human population and obtained representation in government, initiatives such as clean energy, climate change, and sustainable agriculture fell to the wayside. By the time the transhuman population migrated to The Cloud, the humans that remained on Earth were impoverished, pushed to the fringe, barely living in squalor. Resentment festered amongst the endangered human race, and a rebel faction arose, who sought a way to bring down The Cloud.
